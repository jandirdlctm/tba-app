App Guide

TBA Construction — Project Management

tba-app.vercel.app

Welcome! This is your one place to see every job on a map, track what's going on, manage your crew, and keep hours, materials, and photos together per project. This guide walks you through everything — no technical background needed. Open the app in any phone or computer web browser.

The two kinds of accounts

Admin (owner / manager) Worker (subs / crew)
SeesEvery projectOnly projects you assign them
MapAll pinsOnly their assigned pins
Add / edit projectsYesNo
Materials & costsSees and editsNever sees them at all
HoursSees everyone's totalsLogs only their own
PhotosYesYes (on assigned jobs)
Manage workersYesNo

The key idea: workers see only what they need. They can log hours and snap job photos, but they can't see your costs, materials, or each other's hours. That's locked down at the data level — not just hidden on screen.

Signing in

  1. Go to tba-app.vercel.app.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Tap Sign in.

You stay signed in on that device until you log out. To log out: tap the menu (☰, top-left) → Log out.

The map (your home screen)

When you sign in you land on the Project overview map. Every job is a colored pin:

Green = Active (work happening now) Amber = Upcoming (scheduled) Gray = Completed

Things you can do here:

  • Filter: tap All / Active / Upcoming / Completed to show just those pins.
  • Tap a pin for a quick card: name, address, status, work type, and the "Needs" note.
  • View details → on that card opens the full project page.
  • Locate me (◎, bottom-right) recenters the map on where you are.
  • + Add (top-right, admin only) starts a new project.

Adding a new project (admin)

  1. On the map, tap + Add (top-right).
  2. Fill in the form — Project name and Address are required; work type, status, dates, and a scope/needs note are optional.
  3. Tap Save & drop pin.

The app looks up the address and drops the pin automatically. If it can't find the address, it warns you — fix the address and save again, or save anyway and correct it later.

The project detail page

Tap any pin → View details →. This is the full record for one job. Admins see four tabs; workers see three (no Materials).

Overview tab

  • Job stage tracker: Demo / Prep → Build → Finish. This is separate from the green/amber/gray status — it tracks where the physical work is. As admin, tap a stage to move the job along.
  • Details — status, address, work type, dates. Tap Edit to change them (admin).
  • Scope / needs — the working note for the job. Tap Edit to update it.
  • Assigned workers (admin) — covered in "Managing your crew" below.

Materials tab (admin only)

  • Tap + Add material, then enter the item, quantity, unit cost, and status (Needed / Ordered / Received).
  • The app multiplies quantity × cost and keeps a running total at the top.
  • Attach a receipt photo to any line — choose Receipt photo and pick or snap a picture.
  • Edit or delete any line anytime. Workers never see this tab or any of these numbers.

Hours tab

  • Workers see My hours: tap + Add hours, pick the date, enter hours, add an optional note. They can edit/delete their own entries.
  • Admins see the rollup: total hours per worker plus every entry, so you know who logged what.

Photos tab

  • Anyone assigned to the job can add photos — choose Photo, pick or snap an image, add an optional caption.
  • Tap a photo to view it full-size. Delete your own (admins can delete any).
  • Great for progress shots, before/after, and documenting issues on site.

Managing your crew

Add a worker

  1. Menu (☰, top-left) → Manage workers.
  2. Under Add a worker, enter their name, an email, and a temporary password (6+ characters).
  3. Tap Create worker.
  4. Hand the worker that email + password — they sign in with it.
The email doesn't have to be a real inbox — it's just their username. Use something easy like mike@tbacrew.com if you like.

Assign a worker to a job

A worker sees nothing until you assign them to a project.

  1. Open the project → Overview tab → Assigned workers.
  2. Pick the worker from the dropdown → Assign.
  3. To remove them, tap Remove next to their name.

Assign the same worker to as many jobs as you need.

A worker's day

  1. Sign in at tba-app.vercel.app with the email/password you gave them.
  2. Their map shows only their assigned jobs.
  3. Tap a job → View details.
  4. Hours tab → + Add hours to log time for the day.
  5. Photos tab to add job-site pictures.

Simple on a phone, in the field.

Put it on your phone

  • iPhone (Safari): open the site → tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): open the site → tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.

Quick troubleshooting

  • "I can't see any projects." If you're a worker, you haven't been assigned to a job yet — ask your admin. If you're the admin and the map is empty, you haven't added any projects yet.
  • "A new project's pin isn't on the map." The address couldn't be located. Open the project, tap Edit, and correct the address.
  • "I forgot my password." Ask your admin to reset it for you.
  • "A worker says they can see costs." They can't — materials and costs are completely hidden from worker accounts. Double-check they're in their own worker account, not an admin account.
